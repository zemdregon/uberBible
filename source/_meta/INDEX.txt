uberBible source corpus
=======================

English public-domain Protestant Christian Bibles plus original-language texts (Hebrew OT, Greek NT/LXX, Coptic). Non-English modern translations were removed. Catholic editions (Douay-Rheims, Vulgate, WEB Catholic, Petrus Canisius) and Jewish-specific translations (JPS, Leeser, Targum Onkelos) were excluded.
